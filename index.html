Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you get started.

Is VisionImages really free?

This demo has no payment step or account requirement. Hosting, traffic, and advanced features may have costs later when we expand our services.

Are my images uploaded?

No. The image processing in this version runs locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Avoid closing the page before downloads finish.

Will compression always make my file smaller?

No. Results depend on the source image and its format. Some images may not shrink much, and converting a transparent PNG to JPEG fills transparency with white.

Which image formats are supported?

JPG, PNG, and WebP files can be selected. The current version exports JPEG files to keep the compression process consistent.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The layout adapts to mobile screens. Very large images or many files at once can use significant memory on older phones.
